09 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we
begin.

A few useful answers about what to expect when contacting Kawser Hamid and KH Dream Services.

How can I request a consultation?
Select Book Consultation anywhere on this website. Enter your name, email, phone number, address and consultation topic. The form prepares a WhatsApp request that you must send before the team can respond.
Does submitting the form confirm a meeting?
No. Your request is an enquiry only. A meeting date and format are confirmed after the team reviews your message and replies.
Can you assist with Saudi investor licensing?
Investor licensing and business-establishment questions are part of the areas available for discussion. Requirements differ by business activity, applicant circumstances and authority rules; no approval can be promised.
What information should I prepare?
Consider your proposed business activity, goals, existing company details if applicable, target market and any questions about documents or operations. Do not send sensitive documents through the public website form.
Are consultations offered only in Riyadh?
The portfolio is based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Use the booking form to ask about your location and whether a remote conversation may be arranged; availability is confirmed by the team.
Where can I see the company website?
Visit KH Dream Services for company information or use the consultation button to submit a question.

Have a more specific question? A focused consultation request is the best next step.
